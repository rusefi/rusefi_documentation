# 2018 Volkswagen GTI (AU2) L4-2.0L Turbo (CXCB) — ECM pinout

Transcribed from [2018 VW GTI ECU Diagram.pdf](2018%20VW%20GTI%20ECU%20Diagram.pdf) (ALLDATA "Engine Controls" wiring diagram, pages 1–9, diagram IDs 609240–609248).

Engine/Motor Control Module (ECM, Simos 18), located left side of engine compartment. The diagram is image-only and prints no per-pin function names; the "Function (as printed)" column below carries the printed name of the component the wire lands on.

Connectors covered:

| Connector | Pins | Role |
|-----------|------|------|
| T91 | 91 | Vehicle harness: power/relays, grounds, pedal, brake/clutch switches, O2 sensors, radiator-outlet coolant temp, charge air pressure, CAN, fuel tank leak module |
| T105A | 105 | Engine harness: injectors (direct and port), coils, cam actuators, crank/cam sensors, MAP, throttle, knock, oil/fuel pressure, wastegate actuator |

Wire colors are abbreviated as in the source (BLK black, BRN brown, RED, GRN green, YEL yellow, BLU blue, VIO violet, WHT white, GRY grey, ORG orange; NCA = no color available). Unlisted pins are not connected in this diagram. Tnn designators (T14I/T14T, T8Y/T8AC, T17M/T17E, ...) are the in-line coupling points; the same pin number is bridged across each pair.

Pins 31 and 37: the diagram draws two wires into each of these pins. The second wire (BRN/GRN into 31, GRN/YEL into 37) rises from an unnumbered connector slot immediately to the right of the numbered pin and is jogged into it, while pins 32 and 39 are drawn empty. It is likely a rendering quirk of the source and the low-fuel-pressure-sensor wires may really belong to their own pins; treat these two rows as uncertain.

Component locations (pages 4–8): camshaft position sensor 3 — top rear of engine; camshaft position sensor — rear of cylinder head; engine speed sensor — left rear of engine block; engine temperature control actuator — left side of engine block; charge air pressure actuator/position sensor — exhaust manifold assembly; intake manifold sensor — top of intake manifold; intake manifold runner position sensor — front of intake manifold; throttle valve control module — intake manifold inlet; fuel pressure sensor and low fuel pressure sensor — injector fuel delivery line; fuel injectors (both sets) — 1, 2, 3 & 4 cylinders head intake port; exhaust cam actuators A/B — top of each cylinder; knock sensor 1 — left rear of engine block; ignition coils — top of cylinder head.

## Ignition coils, cam actuators, port injectors (pages 6, 9)

Ignition coils w/ power output stage (4-pin): pin 1 BLK → G846 (coil 1), G847 (coil 2), G848 (coil 3), G849 (coil 4); pin 2 = ECM trigger (T105A 76/79/57/62 for coils 1/2/3/4); pin 3 BRN → G673 (left front longitudinal member); pin 4 RED = supply from engine component power supply relay pin 115.

Exhaust cam actuators A/B on each cylinder (2-pin): pin 1 = ECM (T105A 101, 6, 59, 58, 4, 94, 96, 95 for A1, B1, A2, B2, A3, B3, A4, B4); all pin 2 BLK are bussed together to the BLK supply from fuse 5.

Port injectors ("fuel injector 2 cylinders"): pin 2 = ECM via T8Z/T8AA (T105A 25/46/24/45 for cyl 1/2/3/4); pin 1 via T8Z pins 1/3/5/7 (BRN/YEL, BRN/VIO, BRN/GRY, BRN/GRN) joined to the same BLK bus.

## Power supply / relays / fuses (pages 1–4)

Fuse panel B, left side of engine compartment (all hot at all times unless noted):

- **Motronic engine control module power supply relay** — coil pin 42 ← T91-7 (BLK/GRY); contact output pin 41 (RED/BLK) → T91-86, same node as fuse 17 (7.5 A) and ABS control module pin 31 / pin 2.
- **Engine component power supply relay** — coil pin 112 ← T91-8 (VIO); coil pin 111 BLK (or BLK/RED) on the fuse 5 BLK bus; contact pin 113 RED/BLK ← fuse 16 (20 A); contact output pin 115 RED → T14I/T14T pin 5 → ignition coil pin 4 supply.
- **Secondary air injection pump relay** — pin 52 VIO/RED ← T105A-60; pin 51 BLK/RED on the fuse 4 (10 A) BLK/RED bus; pin 11A BLK ← fuse 11 (50 A) → secondary air injection pump motor pin 2 (left front of engine compt); motor pin 1 BRN → G671.
- Fuse 3 (15 A) RED/GRN → T91-5/6.
- Fuse 4 (10 A) BLK/RED → SAI pump relay pin 51; and via T14I pin 8 (BLK/RED) / T14T pin 8 (YEL/BLK) → supply pins of intake manifold runner control valve (pin 2), turbocharger recirculation valve (pin 1) and oil pressure regulation valve (pin 2).
- Fuse 5 (10 A) BLK → engine component power supply relay pin 111; via T14I/T14T pin 4 → exhaust cam actuator pin 2 bus and port injector pin 1 bus.
- Fuse 6 (5 A) GRY/RED → brake lamp switch pin 4.
- Fuse 7 (7.5 A) RED/BLU → coolant shut-off valve pin 1 (M/T) / transmission coolant valve pin 1 (A/T), via T14I/T14T pin 2.
- Fuse 8 (10 A) WHT/RED → oxygen sensor heaters (before-cat sensor pin 4, after-cat sensor pin 1).
- Fuse 9 (20 A) RED/WHT → via T14I/T14T pin 6 to the WHT / RED/WHT supply bus (page 8): EVAP canister purge regulator valve 1 pin 1 (RED/WHT), secondary air injection solenoid valve pin 2 (WHT), exhaust camshaft adjustment valve 1 pin 1 (WHT/GRN), camshaft adjustment valve 1 pin 1 (WHT/GRN), heater support pump pin 2 (RED).
- Fuse 10 (10 A) RED/BLU → via T17M/T17E pin 15 → fuel pump control module pin 3.
- Fuse 16 (20 A) RED/BLK → engine component power supply relay pin 113.

Fuse panel C, left side of dash: fuse 20 (5 A, hot at all times) BLK → fuel tank leak detection control module pin 4; fuse 34 (7.5 A, hot w/ terminal 15 power supply relay energized) BLK/YEL → structure borne sound control module pin 3 (rear of engine compt).

## Fuel pump control module — under right side of rear seat

The fuel pump is not driven by the ECM directly; the module is commanded on T91-9.

| Pin | Wire | Function | Connects to |
|-----|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | BLK | TRANSFER FUEL PUMP | Fuel delivery unit pin 1 (right side of fuel tank) |
| 2 | BLU | TRANSFER FUEL PUMP | Fuel delivery unit pin 5 |
| 3 | RED/BLU | PWR SPLY | Fuse 10 (10 A), via T17M/T17E pin 15 |
| 4 | BRN | GND | G730 (right rear wheel housing) |
| 5 | BLK/BRN | ECM | T91-9, via T17M/T17E pin 13 |

Fuel level sensor (fuel delivery unit pins 4 VIO/WHT, 3 VIO/BLK, 2 BRN/BLU) goes to the instrument cluster (pins 14, 15, 11), not to the ECM. Heater support pump pin 1 BRN → G673. Fuel tank pressure sensor (right "D" pillar) pins 1 BLK/WHT and 3 BRN/WHT go to the fuel tank leak detection control module pins 1 and 5.

## Not legible / uncertain

- T105A pins 31 and 37 each carry a second wire (BRN/GRN and GRN/YEL, low fuel pressure sensor) drawn through an unnumbered slot; see note above. Pins 32 and 39 are drawn with no wire.
- T105A pin 42 is printed as "YEL/GRN (OR GRN/RED)".
- Engine component power supply relay pin 111 is printed as "BLK (OR BLK/RED)".
- No other pins or labels were unreadable.
