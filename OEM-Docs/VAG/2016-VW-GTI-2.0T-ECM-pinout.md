# 2016 Volkswagen GTI (AU1) L4-2.0L Turbo (CXCB) — ECM pinout

Transcribed from [2016-gti-2.0T.pdf](2016-gti-2.0T.pdf) (ALLDATA "Engine Controls" wiring diagram, pages 1–9, sheets 514048–514056).

Engine Control Module (ECM), located left side of engine compartment. Two connectors, T91 (91-pin) and T105 (105-pin), 196 pins total (Simos 18 family layout). Engine code CXCB (EA888 Gen 3, port + direct injection).

Connectors covered:

| Connector | Pins | Role |
|-----------|------|------|
| T91 | 91 | Vehicle harness: power, grounds, relay controls, accelerator pedal, O2 sensors, charge air pressure, brake/clutch/cruise, CAN, transmission signals |
| T105 | 105 | Engine harness: port and direct injectors, ignition coils, throttle, exhaust cam actuators, engine sensors and valves |

Wire colors are abbreviated as in the source (BLK black, BRN brown, RED, GRN green, YEL yellow, BLU blue, VIO violet, WHT white, GRY grey, ORG orange). Unlisted pins are not connected in this diagram. The diagram prints only the wire color at each ECM pin (no function names), so the "Function" column is inferred from the component the wire goes to; "Connects to" gives the component and pin as printed. Many engine-side wires pass through "coupling point" connectors (T14L/T14M left coupling point in engine compartment, T14E/T14F top right coupling point on engine, T8H/T8I top center coupling point on engine, T17O/T17G bottom left coupling point in interior, T10AF/T10AG, T8F/T8G, T6CJ/T6CQ); these are listed as "via".

Other actuator/coil pins: ignition coil pin 4 (RED) is fed from the engine component power supply relay (terminal 115 RED), pin 3 (BRN) goes to G673 (left front longitudinal member), pin 1 (BLK) to G846/G847/G848/G849. Direct injector (fuel injector 2) pin 1 wires (BRN/YEL, BRN/VIO, BRN/GRY, BRN/GRN) are spliced to a common BLK feed, and the exhaust camshaft actuator pin 2 (BLK) wires share the same BLK feed. The second pins of the intake manifold runner control valve, turbocharger recirculation valve and oil pressure regulation valve share a YEL/BLK supply; piston cooling nozzle valve pin 2 is WHT.

## Power supply / relays (pages 1, 3, 4)

Fuse panel B, left side of engine compartment:

- **Motronic engine control module power supply relay** — contact 30 hot at all times; coil terminal → fuse panel B terminal 42 BLK/GRY → T91/7. Contact 87 feeds fuses 3 (15 A, → T91/5,6), 7 (7.5 A), 8 (10 A), 9 (20 A or 10 A), 4 (10 A), 5 (10 A), 6 (5 A) and 10 (10 A).
- **Fuse 17** (7.5 A) hot at all times → 17A/41 RED/BLK → T91/86 and ABS control module pin 31.
- **Fuse 16** (20 A) hot at all times → 16A RED/BLK → engine component power supply relay terminal 113.
- **Engine component power supply relay** (relay & fuse carrier 1, page 4) — terminal 113 RED/BLK in, 115 RED out (ignition coil supply etc.), coil 111 BLK (from fuse 5 BLK feed) and 112 VIO → T91/8.
- **Secondary air injection pump relay** (page 1) — coil terminal 52 VIO/RED → T105/60, terminal 51 BLK/RED; contact feeds the secondary air injection pump motor (left front of engine compt) through fuse 11 (50 A).

Fuse panel C (left front of engine compartment): fuse 20 (5 A, hot at all times, BLK) and fuse 34 (7.5 A, hot w/ terminal 15 power supply relay energized, BLK/YEL) feed the transmission/ABS side of the diagram, not the ECM directly.

Grounds: T91/1,2 → G671 (left front frame member); G673 (left front longitudinal member) for coils, sensors and the DSG/clutch circuits; G730 (right rear wheel housing) for the fuel pump control module.
