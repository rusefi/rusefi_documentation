# 2020 Volkswagen GTI (AU2) L4-2.0L Turbo (DKFA) — ECM pinout

Transcribed from [2020 VW GTI 20t ECU Diagram.pdf](2020%20VW%20GTI%2020t%20ECU%20Diagram.pdf) (ALLDATA "Engine Controls" wiring diagram, pages 1-9, diagram ids 726527-726535).

Engine/Motor Control Module (ECM, Simos 18), located left side of engine compartment. The ECM has two connectors: T91 (vehicle/body side) and T105A (engine side).

Connectors covered:

| Connector | Pins | Role |
|-----------|------|------|
| T91 | 91 | Vehicle harness: power/relays, grounds, pedal, brake/clutch switches, O2 sensors, EVAP leak detection, coolant valves, data bus, start/charge |
| T105A | 105 | Engine harness: injectors (HP + port), coils, cam/crank sensors, throttle, boost/temperature sensors, cam actuators, oil/fuel pressure valves |

Wire colors are abbreviated as in the source (BLK black, BRN brown, RED, GRN green, YEL yellow, BLU blue, VIO violet, WHT white, GRY grey, ORG orange). Unlisted pins are not connected in this diagram. The ALLDATA diagram prints no function names at the ECM pins; the Function column below is derived from the component the wire connects to. Most wires run across several pages of the diagram through numbered page-edge continuations and coupling points (T14x, T17x, T8x, T6x); the destinations were traced through those.

Notes on the engine harness:

- High-pressure fuel injectors (pages 5-6, "fuel injector cylinder n", intake port): each injector's two wires (NCA, not color coded) come through the top right coupling point on engine T8Y/T8AC; the ECM drives both wires of each injector (cyl 1: pins 43 + 65, cyl 2: 2 + 22, cyl 3: 1 + 23, cyl 4: 64 + 85). The injector pin (1 or 2) per ECM wire is not resolvable from the diagram.
- Port fuel injectors ("fuel injector 2 cylinder n", in cylinder head, page 6): through the top center coupling point T8Z/T8AA; the odd T8Z pins (1 BRN/YEL, 3 BRN/VIO, 5 BRN/GRY, 7 BRN/GRN) are spliced together to a BLK supply, so the ECM drives only one wire per injector (cyl 1: pin 25, cyl 2: 46, cyl 3: 24, cyl 4: 45).
- Exhaust cam actuators A/B for cylinders 1-4 (page 6, top of each cylinder): pin 1 is driven by the ECM, pin 2 (BLK) of all eight is bussed to the BLK supply through the left coupling point T14I/T14T 4.
- Ignition coils w/ power output stage (page 9, top of cylinder head): pin 2 = ECM trigger, pin 4 RED = supply (engine component power supply relay), pin 3 BRN = G673, pin 1 BLK = G846 (coil 1), G847 (coil 2), G848 (coil 3), G849 (coil 4).
- The wire color printed at the ECM sometimes differs from the color printed at the far end of the same run (e.g. T105A 28 WHT to engine speed sensor pin 1 GRY/WHT; T105A 35 GRY/WHT to camshaft position sensor 3 pin 2 WHT; T91 32/35/54/55 change color across the T14D/T14F coupling). The ECM-side color is listed in the Wire column.

## Power supply / relays (pages 1, 3, 4)

Fuse panel B, left side of engine compartment:

- **Motronic engine control module power supply relay** — pin 30 hot at all times; pin 85 = T91-7 (BLK/GRY); pin 86 = RED/BLK from fuse 17 (7.5 A, hot at all times; also T91-86 and ABS module pin 31/2); pin 87 feeds fuses 3 (15 A), 7 (7.5 A), 8 (10 A), 9 (20 A), 4 (10 A), 5 (10 A), 6 (5 A), 10 (10 A).
  - Fuse 3 (RED/GRN) -> T91 5/6.
  - Fuse 7 (RED/BLU) -> coolant shut-off valve pin 1 and transmission coolant valve pin 1 (via T14I 2 / T14D 9 - T14T 2 / T14F 9), fuel tank leak detection control module pin 4 (via T17L/T17C 8), T14D 2.
  - Fuse 8 (WHT/RED) -> oxygen sensor heaters (before cat pin 5, after cat pin 1).
  - Fuse 9 (RED/WHT, via T14I/T14T 6) -> heater support pump pin 2, camshaft adjustment valve 1 pin 1 (WHT), exhaust camshaft adjustment valve 1 pin 1 (WHT), evap canister purge regulator valve 1 pin 1, secondary air injection solenoid valve pin 5 (WHT).
  - Fuse 4 (BLK/RED) -> secondary air injection pump relay pins 30/85, engine component power supply relay pin 111 (coil), and via T14I/T14T 8 the YEL/BLK supply to intake manifold runner control valve pin 1, turbocharger recirculation valve pin 1, oil pressure regulation valve pin 2, piston cooling nozzle control valve pin 2 (WHT).
  - Fuse 5 (BLK) -> exhaust cam actuators pin 2 and port injector supply (T8Z 1/3/5/7), via T14I/T14T 4.
  - Fuse 6 (GRY/RED) -> brake lamp switch pin 4.
  - Fuse 10 (RED/BLU) -> fuel pump control module pin 3 (via T17M/T17E 15).
- **Engine component power supply relay** — pin 113 = RED/BLK from fuse 16 (20 A, hot at all times); pin 111 = BLK/RED from fuse 4 (coil supply); pin 112 = T91-8 (VIO, coil control); pin 115 (RED) -> via T14I/T14T 5 to ignition coils pin 4.
- **Secondary air injection pump relay** — pins 30 and 85 from fuse 4 (BLK/RED); pin 86 = T105A-60 (VIO/RED); pin 87 -> fuse 11 (50 A) -> secondary air injection pump motor pin 2 (BLK), left front of engine compartment; motor pin 1 BRN -> G673.
- Fuel pump control module (under right side of rear seat): pin 5 <- T91-9, pin 3 <- fuse 10, pin 4 BRN -> G708, pins 1 (BLK) / 2 (BLU) -> transfer fuel pump in the fuel delivery unit (right side of fuel tank). Fuel level sensor pins 2/3/4 (BRN/BLU, VIO/BLK, VIO/WHT) go to the instrument cluster pins 11/15/14.

Grounds: G673 (left front longitudinal member) — T91 1/2, coils pin 3, brake lamp switch pin 2, clutch position sensor pin 1, T6AB 5; G687 — driving profile selection button; G708 — fuel pump control module; G846-G849 — on ignition coils 1-4.
