## Unresolved readings

- **F06 / F0006U (ABS PUMP SRT)** — the last digit could be 5 or 6. The row sits between F04 and F07.
- **F19, F28, F58, F66, F88** — the last digit could be 8 or 9 (F19, F28, F58), 5, 6 or 8 (F66), or 6, 8 or 9 (F88); neighbouring rows do not narrow it down.
- **Pairs read by row order** — F25/F26, F35/F36, F38/F39, F68/F69, F75/F76, F85/F86, F95/F96 and F98/F99 are told apart only by their ascending order in the table.
- **Relay Alternate codes** — R0813, D838, D893, D2759, D816 and D876 contain 3/5/6/8/9 digits that are not individually legible.
- **Ratings** — 60A (F03, F04) could be read as 50A by shape, and the 15A and 25A entries rely on the same ambiguous "5" glyph. The HVAC wiring diagram for the same vehicle prints fuse 3 as 60 A, and also confirms fuse 13 (40 A), 15 (40 A), 37 (25 A), 49 (10 A), 62 (10 A), 64 (25 A), 99 (10 A), 101 (15 A) and 103 (10 A). The F04 rating has no such confirmation.
