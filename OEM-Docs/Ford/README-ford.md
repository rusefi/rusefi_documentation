# Ford ECU schematic transcriptions

Companion pinouts for the dated 2012-and-newer ECU schematics in this directory. Each document preserves printed pin functions and wire colours, cites the local source and PDF pages, and records power, fuse, relay and ground routing. Unresolved routing and source conflicts are identified in the companion documents.

## Other material

The pre-2012 diagrams, suspension diagrams, CAN traces and standalone wideband-sensor description are outside this ECU transcription set. The Coyote pinout and connector images have no model year printed or established in this directory; they are retained as undated references and are not assigned to a 2012+ vehicle.
