# 2009 Mazda MX-5 Miata NC2 engine controls wiring

Transcribed from [2009 NC2.pdf](2009%20NC2.pdf), **2009 Mazda MX-5 Miata L4-2.0L**, “Vehicle > Powertrain Management > Diagrams > Electrical - Interactive Color (Non OE)”, Engine Controls, pages 1–4. Diagram numbers: 316318, 316319, 316320, 316321.

The tables record the printed wire colors, terminal labels, components, locations, and connections. Destinations across page boundaries are followed through the numbered continuation lines. A dash means **no wire is drawn at that terminal in this diagram**, not a separate claim that the terminal is unused. Component terminal letters are included where printed; unlabeled component terminals are described by their wires. The PDF remains the reference for symbol shapes and physical drawing layout.

Colors are retained as printed: BLK black, BLU blue, BRN brown, GRN green, GRY gray, ORG orange, PNK pink, RED red, VIO violet, WHT white, YEL yellow; LT means light. Slash-separated colors retain their printed order. M/T and A/T identify manual and automatic transmission variants. J/C and NCA are retained as source labels.

## Page 2 power distribution and instrument cluster

Relay & main fuse block: left side of engine compartment. Separate fuse block: behind left end of dash.

| Fuse or relay | Rating / supply condition | Connections shown |
|---|---|---|
| STOP fuse | 10A; hot at all times | RED/BLK to brake switch |
| FUEL PUMP fuse | 15A; hot at all times | RED/GRN to fuel pump relay contact |
| Main relay (EGI) | Contact supply hot at all times | Coil: YEL/GRN from ETV fuse, GRN/YEL to PCM 1Q. Switched output feeds EGI COMP2, EGI COMP1 and EGI INJ fuses |
| EGI COMP2 fuse | 10A | BLK/BLU branches to PCM 1AW and 1BE supply networks |
| EGI COMP1 fuse | 10A | Two BLK/RED branches through pages 3–4 to EGR and solenoid valves |
| EGI INJ fuse | 10A | Two WHT/BLU branches through pages 3–4 to injectors and PCM 2BH |
| ETV fuse | 10A; hot at all times | YEL/GRN to main relay coil and drive-by-wire relay contact |
| ROOM fuse | 15A; hot at all times | BLU/RED to PCM 1BA and instrument cluster 1C |
| Ignition relay (IGNITION) | Contact supply hot at all times | Coil: GRN from separate ENGINE fuse 7.5A; BLK to J/C 1, G1. Contact output supplies ENGINE fuse 15A |
| ENGINE fuse, relay & main fuse block | 15A | BLK/WHT to PCM 1AY, fuel pump relay coil, power distribution system, ignition coils, condenser and oxygen sensors |
| ENGINE fuse, separate fuse block | 7.5A; hot in ON or START | GRN to ignition relay coil |
| Drive-by-wire relay (ETV) | In relay & main fuse block | Coil: BLK/BLU from EGI COMP2 fuse; WHT/BLK to PCM 1AX. Contacts: YEL/GRN from ETV fuse; BLU to PCM 1BF |
