# 2020 Mazda MX-5 Miata Club ECU wiring

Transcribed from [2020-mx5-ECU.pdf](2020-mx5-ECU.pdf). Source header: PRODEMAND; YMMS: 2020 Mazda MX-5 Miata Club; Engine: 2.0L Eng; Aug 30, 2022. VIN, License and Odometer fields are blank. Section: **ENGINE PERFORMANCE > 2.0L SKYACTIV**. Figures 1–5 are titled “2.0L SKYACTIV, Engine Performance Circuit” and numbered 679473–679477. PDF page 6 is blank.

The tables retain the printed terminals, wire colors, component names, locations and connector references. Connections spanning figures are traced through their numbered continuation lines. A dash means **no wire is drawn at that terminal in this diagram**, not a separate claim that the terminal is unused. The PDF remains the reference for symbol shapes and physical drawing layout.

Colors: BLK black, BLU blue, BRN brown, GRN green, GRY gray, PNK pink, RED red, TAN tan, VIO violet, WHT white, YEL yellow; LT means light. Slash-separated colors retain the printed order. M/T and A/T identify manual and automatic transmission variants. J/C and NCA are retained as source labels. A connector reference such as C-01/J means connector C-01, terminal J. Arrows to other systems are recorded with the source system name, without assigning an unprinted signal name.
